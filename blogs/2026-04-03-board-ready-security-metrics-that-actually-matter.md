TL;DR Board-ready security metrics translate technical capabilities into financial risk and business outcomes. Boards need visibility across three dimensions: risk exposure, incident response capability and governance compliance. Application security metrics contribute meaningful data points to these broader assessments, helping security leaders present a complete picture of organizational risk.
